AllStructures QuickBooks connection. Last updated 16 October 2026.

What this is

AllStructures is a private connection between QuickBooks Online and Claude, run by Unmasking Potential for All Structures Ltd. It is not offered to the public.

Using it

The business decides what Claude is asked to do. Anything Claude prepares, such as a draft estimate, should be checked by the business before it is sent or relied on.

Your data

The business keeps ownership of its QuickBooks data. How data is handled is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Ending use

The business can stop using the connection at any time by disconnecting it in QuickBooks Online.
