Privacy Policy

AllStructures QuickBooks connection. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Who we are

The AllStructures connection is run by Unmasking Potential. You can contact us at rob@unmaskingpotential.co.uk.

Who it is for

The connection is used only by All Structures Ltd, for its own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

When the business connects its QuickBooks company, the connection can read accounting records such as customers, suppliers, items, estimates, invoices and tax settings. When asked, it can create or update records such as draft estimates.

Where the data goes

The connection runs on the business's own computer. Records are passed to Claude, made by Anthropic, under the business's own Claude account, so that Claude can answer questions and prepare work. Unmasking Potential does not run a server that stores QuickBooks data.

The sign in tokens that QuickBooks issues are kept in a settings file on the business's own computer.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or share QuickBooks data with anyone else.
  • We do not use QuickBooks data for advertising.
  • We do not keep copies of QuickBooks data on our own systems.

Disconnecting

The business can disconnect at any time from the Apps section of QuickBooks Online. Deleting the settings file on its computer removes the stored sign in tokens.

Your rights

You can ask what data is held about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing rob@unmaskingpotential.co.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
