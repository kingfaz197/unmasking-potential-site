AllStructures QuickBooks connection

AllStructures is a private connection between QuickBooks Online and Claude. It is built for one business, All Structures Ltd, and is not offered to the public.

It lets Claude read records in that business's QuickBooks company and, when asked, prepare items such as draft estimates.
